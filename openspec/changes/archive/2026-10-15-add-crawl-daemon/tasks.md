- [x] 5.2 Implement the worker pool: `max_parallel_jobs` workers on a FIFO queue, per-job `ParseCache` shared by the daemon's `SharedClientFetcher`, `CachingParser` and `StorageWriter` (no library fallback components), and final status rules (seed failure, then `failed`; exception, then `failed` with `Type: message`; cancellation, then `cancelled`). - [x] 5.3 Implement `cancel()` (queued: mark cancelled and the worker skips it; running: cancel the task, keep its pages; finished: `ConflictError`), `shutdown()` (cancel running jobs, leave queued ones, close the client after the workers stop) and `recover()` (`mark_interrupted()` then re-enqueue queued jobs by submission time). Verify with tests covering each spec scenario under "Clients can cancel", "Clients can stop the daemon" and "Jobs survive a daemon restart", including a simulated crash (a job left `running` in storage, then a new scheduler)
